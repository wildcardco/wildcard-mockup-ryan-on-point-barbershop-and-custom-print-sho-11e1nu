The Shop

Hometown Hustle, On Sauk Trail

On Point isn't a franchise salon — it's a neighborhood shop in Sauk Village where the barber chair and the print press sit side by side. That combination didn't come from a business plan; it came from doing two things well and doing them for the same community.

Men and families from across the south suburbs come through for reliable, sharp cuts. Local teams, churches, and small businesses come through for custom-printed gear. A lot of folks come through for both — because when your barber can also print your brand, one stop covers looking sharp and repping yours.

Walk-in friendly. Community-rooted. Built on repeat local trust, one cut and one print run at a time.
